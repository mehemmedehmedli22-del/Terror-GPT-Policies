Terror GPT Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Information We Process

Terror GPT processes Discord messages and user identifiers needed to provide its commands and AI chat features.

AI Services

Messages sent to the AI chat feature are processed by Groq to generate responses. Please review Groq's privacy policy to understand how its services handle data.

Data Storage

Chat history is kept temporarily in the bot's running memory while a chat session is active. It is not intentionally saved to a permanent database by the bot code. Restarting the bot may clear this history.

Data Sharing

Messages sent for AI responses are shared with the AI service provider. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information to the bot.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the bot developer through the official Discord application or support channel.
